Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the pad. Tap the little map in the corner (or press M) to see the whole world. Follow the dirt paths through gaps in the trees to reach new areas; the minimap shows where you are. Weaken a wild creature before throwing an orb to raise your catch odds. Step into a gym's door to challenge its leader, or climb down the hole beside it to explore the caves. Step into a purple teleport hole to travel anywhere fast. Walk into a trainer with a red ! to battle for coins, then spend them at the blue-striped shops. Walk into a crystal to smash it for coins. Swim in lakes and ponds to find water creatures. Walk into a lava pool in Ember Hollow and its bubbles will teleport you to Lava Island. Climb Frostspire Mountain on Crystal Peak to reach a legendary at the top. Win all 9 badges to open the BOSS building in Sunny Meadow.
